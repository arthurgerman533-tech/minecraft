# PaxVanila privacy policy

This policy covers the PaxVanila website and mobile app (Android and iOS). Last updated 18 November 2026.

## What data we collect

None. The app and the website need no account and do not collect, store or share personal data.

## What stays on your device

- All texts (lore, rules, mechanics, guides) are built into the app.
- Your theme (light or dark) and language choice are stored only on your device.

## Third-party services

- To show the server status, the app sends the game server address to the public service api.mcsrvstat.us. No data about you is sent.
- The website loads fonts from Google Fonts.
- Links to Discord and the map open in an external browser or app, where their own rules apply.

There are no ads, analytics or trackers.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Contact

Ask your questions in the PaxVanila Discord server.
