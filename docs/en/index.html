    📲App for your phone

    The site installs on your phone as an app and works offline. Android also has an APK.

    ⬇️ Download APK for Android ⬇️ Backup APK link

    🤖 Android

    1. Open this site in Chrome. If you came from a link in Discord or Telegram, tap ⋮ → “Open in Chrome”: the built-in browser cannot install apps.
    2. Tap “Install” above. If nothing appears: menu ⋮ → “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
    3. Or download the APK, open the file and allow “Install unknown apps” for your browser.
    4. If it says “App not installed”, uninstall the old PaxVanila and install again.

    📱 Xiaomi, Redmi, POCO

    1. Download in Chrome, not in Mi Browser or Telegram: they often cut or block downloads. If the main link fails, tap “Backup APK link”.
    2. Open Files (File Manager) → Downloads → PaxVanila.apk.
    3. The phone will ask about unknown sources: Settings → turn on “Allow from this source” for Chrome or File Manager, then Back.
    4. If the Xiaomi installer scans the app and warns you, tap “Install anyway” or “Continue”. If it asks for a Mi account, sign in or install via Chrome.
    5. If it says “Blocked by Play Protect”: More details → Install anyway. If that fails: Play Store → avatar → Play Protect → ⚙ → temporarily turn off “Scan apps”.
    6. Still no luck? Install the site as an app: Chrome → menu ⋮ → Install app. It works on any phone.

    🍏 iPhone and iPad

    1. Open the site in Safari (Chrome on iPhone also works, via the same Share button).
    2. Tap Share (the square with an up arrow) at the bottom of the screen.
    3. Scroll down, choose “Add to Home Screen”, then “Add”.
    The season

    Every week unlocks something new

    The world border grows a little every day. The count starts from the server launch.

    World 1 · Vanilla

    World map

    The Monolith at the centre, the world border week by week and the powers of the lore. Drag the map with the mouse or two fingers, zoom with the wheel, a pinch or the buttons. On a phone it is easier to open it full screen with the ⛶ button.

    World 2 · The real Earth

    Map of the Earth

    The server has two worlds. The first is vanilla (map above), the second is built on the real Earth. Here the powers stand where their prototypes were: Rome, Carthage, Athens, Gaul, Britain, Germania, Scandinavia, Dacia, the Great Steppe and Crimea. Tap a power to see who it was in history.

    The world beneath the world

    Nether map

    One block in the Nether equals eight blocks above. Here are the portals of the powers, the Forerunner fortresses, the piglin bastions and the Great Gates beneath the Monolith. Tap a marker to read what is there.
