# PaxVanila: The City of Dubai

*The full account of "Record E" from "Old Records". The city by the Sand Sea, as ZAG533 filmed it*

> Warden-7 played this tape more often than any other. Two hours without a single fight. *"In the Front Chronicle ZAG533 burns villages. Here he hangs signs so people don't get lost. It is the same man. I want you to see both records."*

---

## What this record is

- **Length:** about two hours, a live broadcast. Viewer offerings run in the corner the whole time: 2, 3, 4, 5 rubles, and once 12 euros. Warden-7 calls them **"copper from the ether"**: people watched a city being built and paid for it, though they could hold nothing in their hands.
- **World:** the **World of the Sand City**, one of the five worlds of "Old Records". On the PaxVanila map it lies beyond the southern border. It has its own generator seed, but the builder is the same man: the record opens on the world list, and the first chat line is **"ZAG533 joined the game"**.
- **Almost no voice.** The builder is silent. Somewhere in the middle someone calls his name from far away. Everything we know about the city is written on his signs.
- **The world saves itself.** Chat keeps saying *"Game saved"* and *"Player idle timeout is 10 minutes"*. The city lived by the clock: whoever stood idle for ten minutes was thrown out of the world.

---

## Where the city stands

Dubai stands on the seashore in the middle of a vast desert. The map from the record shows it from above:

- **The yellow continent of the Sand Sea** covers almost the whole map.
- **Green forest** lies to the north and west, with winding rivers.
- **The blue sea** cuts into the desert as a great bay. Along the shore by the city runs an even comb: **piers**, one for every quarter.
- **Islands.** Sandy islands lie in the bay; one carries a mark like a cross.
- **Green markers** stand where people lived. The red marker by the piers is the builder himself.

> *"They built a city where there is no tree and no grass. All the wood came by sea. That is why every quarter has a pier,"* Warden-7.

---

## The quarters

### 1. The Twisted Tower

The first thing in the record is the night desert and a **tower twisted into a spiral** of prismarine and white quartz, each floor turned a little from the one below. From afar it seems to rotate slowly.

Two straight lines run from it across the sand: a **prismarine road** and a **row of torches**, so the city can be found from the sea at night. A **purple portal** stands next to the tower. The record never shows where it leads.

### 2. The Pyramid

Beside the tower stands a **sandstone pyramid** with steps and a central entrance, a lantern hanging over its top. The pyramid is older than the city. Warden-7 thinks the builder found it and chose to set his city next to it. *"Old walls tell you where to live."*

### 3. The City Warehouse

The largest building in the record. Above the door hangs a sign ZAG533 types live, letter by letter:

> **WAREHOUSE OF THE CITY OF DUBAI**
> **NO ENTRY**
> **+TRIAL AND +KILL**

Inside:

- **A wall of item frames.** Floor to ceiling, each frame holds a sample: emerald, gold, iron, lapis, quartz, clay, gunpowder, brick, carrot, leather. A chest sits under each frame. No need to open a hundred chests to find iron: look at the wall.
- **A floor in two blues**, dark blue and turquoise concrete laid in squares like palace tiles.
- **A white pillar** in the middle with a stand on it, for whatever the city values most.
- **Shulkers.** Orange shulker boxes stand around the hall: chests you can carry away whole.

### 4. The Blue Hall and the offices

Past the warehouse begins the part of the building where people live and work: dark concrete walls, blue square floors, **sea-lantern lamps on white poles**, **sofas of prismarine stairs** (chat honestly says *"You are now sitting"*, the first city with seats made for people), **glass partitions** dividing offices with birch and dark oak doors, and **the great map** of dozens of sheets laid on a table, from which ZAG533 planned the city.

### 5. The Green Office

One door carries the only kind sign in the city:

> **TO THE GREEN OFFICE**
> **ENTRY ALLOWED**
> **THERE IS A BAR**

Everyone was let in here. Warden-7 says this was the real heart of the city: not the warehouse, not the White House, but a bar you could walk into uninvited.

### 6. The Farms and the Private Office

Two signs side by side over another door:

> **WAREHOUSE ENTRANCE. TRESPASSERS: KILL AND +TRIAL**
>
> **ENTRANCE TO THE FARMS AND PRIVATE OFFICE. TRESPASSERS: KILL AND +TRIAL**

Below ground: a **sandstone and stone-brick hall** with red and gray brick tiles, glowstone and a crafting table; a **machine hall** of pistons and rails with redstone, a farm that runs itself; and a **lever signed "off/on pumpkin farm"**.

### 7. The White House

Near the end ZAG533 sets a new sign over the tower door:

> **WHITE HOUSE**
> **> OF PLAYER ZAG533 <**

His own home, right above the City Warehouse. The master of the city lives above its stores. *"He didn't hide where he lived. He signed his house with his name. Later, in the Chronicle, he signs his camera the same way,"* Warden-7.

### 8. The Obsidian Rooms

Almost a third of the record ZAG533 spends under the sand, breaking and placing **obsidian**, laying **gray concrete** and water, cutting steps into sandstone. The result: blind black rooms without windows. Warden-7 knows three theories: a **vault** (obsidian can't be blown up), a **prison** (Day 517 already had a cage with fire), or a **shelter** to wait out an explosion.

> *"I think it was all three. A city without law builds walls that cannot be broken. Then we invented law, and such walls were no longer needed."*

### 9. The residential quarter

On the outskirts stands a **multi-storey house of sandstone and planks** with rows of windows and a porch. The only building with no threatening sign. People simply lived here.

---

## The "Kill and Trial" law

Three signs out of six say the same: **"trespassers: kill and +trial"**. It is the **first written law** in the world before PaxVanila, and the order matters: first **kill** the trespasser on the spot, then **try** him, already dead.

*"First kill, then judge. They really wrote it that way. In PAX we swapped the words: first the trial, then everything else. That is the difference between their world and ours,"* Warden-7.

---

## The Heavy Revolver

On a warehouse pillar, on a framed pedestal, lies an unusual weapon. When picked up, the words **"Heavy revolver"** light up on screen. No other record has such a weapon. Warden-7 believes it was the **city master's weapon**, a sign of power like a sceptre. It was never carried into battle (in the Chronicle ZAG533 fights with sword and shield); it was kept on display for all to see. Where it is now, no one knows.

---

## The last frame

The record does not end in the city. ZAG533 in diamond armor gets into a **boat** and sails into the open sea at dawn. The camera watches from behind until the city drops below the horizon. Then the screen goes dark. There are no more records from Dubai. The World of the Sand City went dark like the other four. ZAG533 next appears in another world, in "Day 517" with the stone village and Telorfind, then in the Front Chronicle and the Far Sector.

> *"He built a city and sailed away. I don't know why. Maybe the city got too small. Maybe he went to find a new village. A year later he found one, and Telorfind was already there,"* Warden-7.

## What PAX took from it

| In Dubai | In PaxVanila |
|---|---|
| Frame wall above chests | A market and warehouse in every capital: goods visible without opening chests |
| A pier for every quarter | Ports (Ports datapack) and sea trade |
| "Kill and +trial" | The PAX law reversed: trial first, punishment after. Killing outside war is a crime |
| Obsidian rooms against TNT | The "anti-ruin" law: a city can't be razed, so no need to hide in obsidian |
| The Green Office with a bar | Neutral places open to all: the Tower at 0, 0 |
| White House above the warehouse | A nation's treasury is kept apart from the ruler's house |
| Heavy revolver on a pedestal | National relics that are shown, not carried into battle |

## Hooks for the server and the mod

Ideas, not finished mechanics.

**Minecraft:**
- **"Sand Sea" event.** When the world border grows, place the ruins of the Twisted Tower and pyramid somewhere in a desert, with a "Warehouse of the City of Dubai" sign and an empty pedestal inside.
- **"Heavy Revolver" relic** for the Arcanum system. No combat damage, but the nation holding it in its capital gains reputation.
- **"Dubai" heritage focus** for a desert people: port trade bonus and a discount on frame warehouses.

**HOI4:**
- **Idea "Kill and Trial":** +stability, −war support for neighbours, +resistance suppression. Removed by the decision "Trial First".
- **Decision "White House":** move the capital to a desert port city.

- **The cross mark on the island.** Treasure, grave, or a mapmaker's slip?
- **The obsidian rooms.** Vault, prison or shelter?
- **The Heavy Revolver.** Where is it now?
- **Why did ZAG533 sail away** from the city he built so long?
