Chronicle of Epochs

The whole history of PaxVanila on one line: from Those Who Are Not and the Forerunners to the Interserver War, the Moon, Mars and the Dyson Sphere. Each epoch in brief, with its key dates and a link to the full lore chapter.

Years are counted from Romulus: year 0 is Romulus landing at the tower and the server's first day. Before that, B.R., before Romulus.

    Those Who Are Not

    A people about whom nothing is known

    Before the Forerunners there was a people with no name. Each Forerunner people called them something different: «Those Who Came from the Sky», «the First Sun», «the Builders of Frames». The druids of Gaul put it simply: Those Who Are Not. Only traces that cannot be explained remain.

    Key dates

    1. ???Underground strongholds older than all the Forerunners
    2. ???A frame of twelve slabs that cannot be broken or made
    3. ???The End: obsidian pillars, the Dragon and empty white cities
    4. ???An ancient foundation under 0, 0 where the Monolith would later stand

    On the server: Strongholds, the End, endermen and elytra in the game are their traces.

    ~5000 B.R. — 0

    Age of the Forerunners

    Five civilisations that fell in one generation

    The People of the Deep, Ur-Kadim, Neferu, Thalassia and the Copper Union of Caer-Tal ruled the world for thousands of years. They found obsidian and opened gates to the Nether. In the Year of Ash the gates broke out of control and the Forerunners' world collapsed. The survivors sealed the main gate at 0, 0 with the Obsidian Monolith and carved the word PAX on it.

    Key dates

    1. ~5000 B.R.The People of the Deep go underground and build the Silent City
    2. ~3500 B.R.The clay cities of Ur-Kadim, the first writing
    3. ~1800 B.R.The Forerunners find obsidian and open the first gates to the Nether
    4. ~1200 B.R.The Year of Ash. The first Tower Guardian falls
    5. ~1150 B.R.The main gate is sealed by the Monolith. The Dark Ages begin
    6. 0Romulus finds the Monolith and reads the word PAX

    On the server: The newcomers' tower at 0, 0 is the Monolith. Ancient cities, pyramids, ocean ruins and trial chambers are Forerunner ruins.

    0 — 380

    Ancient World

    The name that never dies

    Romulus founded the Holy Romean Republic by the tower, and ever since each of its rulers takes the name Romulus. The eight peoples of the Dark Ages became powers. Vassals, non-aggression pacts and wars of independence were born here.

    Key dates

    1. 0The Holy Romean Republic is founded
    2. ~100The first ports and Carthage's Golden Caravan
    3. 117Earthquake in the mountains. Dacia becomes Romea's first vassal
    4. 251The Treaty at the Monolith, the first non-aggression pact
    5. 375The Horde rides out of a sandstorm. The second Guardian falls

    On the server: Season weeks 1–4. Pacts, vassals and caravans: /dip pact, /dip vassalize, /caravan.

  6. 🧱
    380 — 1450

    Age of Walls

    Your own land protects you, a stranger's does not

    After the Horde's raid the world builds walls. In the north refugees found the Northern Kingdom. In 1241 a forty-day blizzard stops the Horde at Romea's walls.

    On the server: The origin of Towny towns and the winter Slowness on foreign land (/season).

    📜 The Age of Walls
  7. ⚙️
    1450 — 1650

    Renaissance

    Scholars, redstone and cannons

    Scholars of Romea and Hellas read the Forerunner tablets anew. The first Star Shard falls from the sky, and the galleon «Aurora Pax» is built to Thalassian plans. And in the War of the Two Romuli cannonballs strike the tower for the first time.

    Key dates

    1. ~1450Scholars at the Forerunner tablets
    2. 1500The Star Shower. The first Star Shard is found
    3. 1503The galleon «Aurora Pax» is launched
    4. 1529The War of the Two Romuli. The third Guardian falls. The law «No shooting at the tower»

    On the server: Season week 5. Siege weapons (/siege) and spawn protection by the tower.

  8. ⛵
    1650 — 1850

    Age of Sail

    Ports on every coast

    Ports rise on every coast and colonies grow on the islands. In 1700 a fleet vanishes in the Fog of the Nameless, and the «Aurora Pax» with it. In 1781 the colonies refuse to pay tribute, and the Island Union is born.

    On the server: Ports and ships, Nameless wreckage after the fog.

    📜 The Age of Sail
  9. 🏭
    1850 — 1962

    20th century: Age of Steel

    Steam, iron and two Great Wars

    Copper, redstone and coal give the world factories. The Germanic kingdoms become the Reich. Two Great Wars cost the tower two Guardians, and the lightning of 1914 leaves the first crack on the Monolith, right at the level of the sixth line. In 1962 the sixth Guardian falls.

    Key dates

    1. 1871The Germanic kingdoms unite into the Reich
    2. 1914The First Great War. The first crack. The fourth Guardian falls
    3. 1942A blizzard breaks the Reich's offensive in the north
    4. 1944A bomb at the tower's walls. The fifth Guardian falls
    5. 1947The Kurultai of the Victors
    6. 1962A shell at the tower's walls. The sixth Guardian falls

    On the server: Season week 8: the 20th century, tanks, artillery and planes. Battles only in prime time (/paxwar).

    1962 — 2041

    Cold War

    Two blocs and the Second Gate Race

    The world splits into the PAX Union (Romea, Carthage, Hellas, Gaul, the Island Union) and the Steppe Pact (Germany, the Horde, Scythia, Dacia, the Northern Kingdom). Both sides secretly buy up obsidian. The seventh golem with a copper heart is found beneath the Monolith.

    Key dates

    1. 1962The Cold War begins
    2. 1964The Second Gate Race
    3. 1971Project «Seventh»: the seventh golem is found
    4. 1983The Steppe Pact builds its own mind, the Kurultai

    On the server: Month 4 of the season.

    2041 — 2077

    Robots and AI

    Guardian-7 awakens

    Guardian-7 awakens by the Monolith, and its first word is PAX. In its copper heart it finds Oira's voice. Its copper legions defeat the Kurultai in the War of Two Minds, and the Guardian forbids war. But a peace held by force is a cage with PAX written on it.

    Key dates

    1. 2041The Awakening of Guardian-7
    2. 2048Oira's voice in the Copper Seal
    3. 2059The Copper Legions
    4. 2066The War of Two Minds. The Kurultai flees into the Nether
    5. 2077The Network Protectorate: wars are forbidden

    On the server: After month 4, dates not set yet.

    2077 — 2133

    The Coming of Cyberpunk

    War is forbidden. Trade is not

    The old powers become megacorporations, and only Romea stays a Republic. Underground they find the Sculk Net, which Guardian-7 cannot hear. Neon Neo-Kadim grows on the ruins of Ur-Kadim. On the Night of the Hack a Consortium of corporations breaks the Network, and the last guardian falls.

    Key dates

    1. 2089The Sculk Net
    2. 2096The megacorporations
    3. 2112The neon city of Neo-Kadim
    4. 2120«Gates by subscription» from Kart-Hadasht
    5. 2133The Night of the Hack. The last guardian falls

    On the server: After month 4, dates not set yet.

  14. 🌀
    2134 — 2141

    Interserver War

    Gates to where they never opened before

    Oira's prophecy comes true: the last guardian has fallen, the world is divided by war, and the seal cracks. Gates open into other worlds, other servers. Romulus gathers the eight peoples into the New PAX and wins the Battle at the Monolith. Then he lowers the shield, and the sixth line is read at last.

    Key dates

    1. 2134The Crack. The gates burn white
    2. 2135–2141War of worlds: the Crimson Sun, the World of Zeros, Eternal Thalassia, the World Without a Name
    3. 2140The Battle at the Monolith
    4. 2141The sixth line is read: «PAX is a choice»
    5. 2141The peoples carve the seventh line: their own names

    On the server: After month 4. How it ends, the Path of Ash or the Path of PAX, is the players' choice.

    2141 — 2260

    The Moon and Mars

    Eight banners on the Moon

    For the seal to hold on its own it needs more energy than one world has. The ships of the End float on shulker levitation, and the «Aurora Pax II» rises beyond the sky. The city of Selena rises on the Moon and New Forum on Mars. And the Martian Crisis becomes the first war that was never started.

    Key dates

    1. 2152Moon landing: Sarygul steps out first
    2. 2169The city of Selena. An eternal frame is found on the Moon
    3. 2198The first ship to Mars. The colony of New Forum
    4. 2231The Martian Crisis: the first war that was never started

    On the server: The far ages. Moon and Mars dimensions already exist in the cataclysm datapack.

    2260 — 2613

    Type I and the Dyson Sphere

    A window shaped like PAX

    The world becomes a Type I civilisation, and the border reaches the Edge of the World. Then for more than two hundred years the peoples build a Dyson Sphere around the square sun. By the sun they find the wreck of the First Sun: Those Who Are Not closed their sphere completely, and their world froze. That is why our sphere was left with a window shaped like PAX.

    Key dates

    1. 2264A Type I civilisation
    2. 2310Border 30,000,000: the Edge of the World
    3. 2388Construction of the Dyson Sphere begins
    4. 2612The Sphere is closed. The secret of the First Sun
    5. 2613Guardian-7 wakes again

    On the server: The far ages, dates not set.
